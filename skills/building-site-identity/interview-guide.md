# Interview Guide (45 minutes, recorded)

A conversational script for the operator. One recorded call with the person (or an organization's founder or director) usually answers most of the questionnaire, in their own voice. Give Claude the transcript afterwards; it drafts the profile and asks only about the gaps.

**Before the call**

- Send the person the "Before we talk" note below.
- Record with their permission (Zoom, Google Meet, a phone recorder or a meeting assistant). Ask for the transcript, not just a summary: their exact words are the point.
- Have Round 0 answered already (site type, consent, approver).

**Before we talk (send this to the person)**

> We'll talk for about 45 minutes so the website sounds like you and gets the facts right. You can skip any question, and nothing goes on the site until you've read it and said yes. If you'd rather not share something publicly, say so and it stays out. Have a couple of things you've written handy if you can (a bio, a post, a talk).

---

## The call

Keep it a conversation. Follow their energy; the order matters less than covering each block. Questions in *italics* are the follow-ups to use when an answer is thin.

### 1. Opening (3 min)

- Thank them; restate the promises: skip anything, nothing published without their OK, they'll review everything.
- "How would you like your name to appear on the site? Full name, first name only, a pen name?" *(Anonymity level, Round 1.1.)*

### 2. Who you are now (7 min)

- "Tell me about your life these days: what you do, what a good day looks like."
- "If someone met you at a conference, how would you describe your work in one sentence?"
- *"Where are you based, and who do you work with or reach?"*

### 3. Your story (12 min) — people with lived experience, advocates

- "Share as much or as little of your story as you're comfortable having on the site. Where would you like to start?"
- "Were there moments when things turned? What happened, and who or what helped?"
- *"What does recovery look like for you today? What keeps it going?"*
- "Is there anything you'd like to keep private: substances, diagnoses, dates, people in your life?"
- "Your recovery date: share it, share the year, or leave it out?"

**Clinicians instead:** "What drew you to this work? How do you describe your approach to a new client? Any moments in your training or career that shaped you?" Then: "Do you share any personal lived experience publicly? Totally your call."

**Organizations instead:** "How did the organization start? Who was there, and what was the moment it began? What are you proudest of?"

### 4. The people you serve (8 min)

- "Who do you most want this site to reach? Picture one person: where are they, what are they feeling?"
- "What questions do people ask you over and over?" *(Get 5–10, in their words.)*
- "After reading something on your site, what's the one thing you'd love them to do?"

### 5. What you believe (7 min)

- "What do you wish more people understood about recovery or mental health?"
- "Where do you stand on [medication for addiction treatment / harm reduction / 12-step and alternatives / faith], or would you rather present the evidence and stay neutral?" *(Pick the ones that matter for their work.)*
- "Is there anything you'd never want your name attached to?"

### 6. How you sound (5 min)

- "Which words do you use for yourself and your journey? Any words you can't stand?"
- "Three words for how you want readers to feel?"
- "Humor, swearing, faith language: yes, no, sometimes?"

### 7. Credentials and practical details (3 min)

- Certifications or licenses (type, state), training, books, talks, awards.
- Services or programs, prices or "free", booking or donation links.
- "Who should review articles before they go live?"

### 8. Close (2 min)

- "Anything I didn't ask that you'd want on the site?"
- Next steps: they'll get a Google Doc to review; nothing is published before they approve it.
- Ask for photos and writing samples by email.

## After the call

2. Ask Claude to draft the profile from it (building-site-identity, Step 2).
3. Send the person the Open Questions list and the Google Doc for review.
